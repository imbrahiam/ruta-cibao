# Ruta Cibao logo exploration

Four independent AI-generated concept PNGs. These are exploration references, not production artwork. The final SVGs were reconstructed from authored geometry, not traced.

## Selection: 01 — three-way junction

Three broad connected roads read as a street network without relying on lane markings. At 16 px the 144/1024 street width is 2.25 px; generous blue spaces separate all three arms. The asymmetry avoids a generic plus sign, a play triangle, or Link's chevron. The final construction regularizes the concept into two constant-width circular bands, merging without a seam. Ends are cropped by the circular boundary.

02 loses its lane detail at small sizes and can read as an S. 03 is clear but resembles a generic cross. 04 is usable but its diagonal arm suggests directional movement more strongly than 01.
