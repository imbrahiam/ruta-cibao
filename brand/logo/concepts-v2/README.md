## Selected: 01 — winding road in perspective

The wide foreground, narrowing upper end and dark asphalt give 01 the most immediate road reading. The three isolated white marks strengthen that reading without dividing the blue circle into ball-like panels. 02 resembles a typographic S; 03 has a pinched vanishing point that disappears at favicon size; 04 is clean but offers less of the requested receding-road character.

The final redraw softens 01's severe perspective and uses three long dashes rather than its five short ones. Its top stays 144 units wide, avoiding a disappearing tip. At 16/32 px the centreline becomes a 68-unit continuous stripe, approximately one pixel at 16 px, with flat inset ends. No edge lines: these compete with the centreline at small sizes. This is an optical simplification of the selected concept, not a downscaled copy of the generated raster.

The production curve is authored from two cubic Bézier segments. The export script constructs filled offset contours, including the markings; there are no raster embeds or live SVG strokes/text in the new glyph.
